# Archived marketing calendars

These `marketing-calendar/1` documents are the Quorum-era 30-day plans for devShark (`marketingshark.json`) and DNESKAi (`caught-up.json`), 4 October to 2 November 2026. They are frozen: nobody updates them.

OwnDashboard's own plan (`marketing_dneskai_plan`, `marketing_devshark_plan`) decides what publishes. `/api/marketing/calendars` keeps the entries here only for dates before today, for the tip citations IG TIPS counts, and takes everything from today on from the plan. See `src/lib/marketing-calendars-plan.ts`.
