# Czech developer job research

Researched 2026-11-06. This public register holds paraphrased summaries and profile-match assessments against the profile on [lukaskouril.dev](https://lukaskouril.dev); private cover letters stay in the owner account. Scores are not hiring probabilities. This file is generated from `src/lib/jobs/czech-research.ts` and `src/lib/jobs/czech-companies.ts`; edit the data, not the tables.

Stack /40 + experience /25 + domain /15 + location /10 + language /10. A mandatory language outside the profile caps the total at 49%; another documented mandatory barrier (degree, clearance, on-site outside Prague) caps it at 59%. Priority: at least 75%, current research and no flagged barrier. B2B and contract roles count, because freelance work is an option. Recheck every original before applying.

74 roles at 66 companies; 41 are priorities on the research date. Sources: StartupJobs, Jobs.cz and Prace.cz, Cocuma, JobStack, NoFluffJobs, LinkedIn guest search, EU Remote Jobs and employer applicant-tracking boards. Each listing page loaded on the research date without a closed notice. Posting dates appear only where the source showed one; LinkedIn may show a refreshed date. Duplicate copies of the same vacancy are kept as aliases of one role.
