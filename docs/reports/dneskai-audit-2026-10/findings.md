## Cost and speed

Local measured rendering: a seven-slide fixture deck took 720.5 ms cold, 503.8–521.7 ms warm and produced 820,252 JPEG bytes (Apple M2; rendering map and handoff measurements). Five kits extrapolate to about 4.1 MB and 2.5–3.6 CPU seconds per post. Uploads, storage requests, cold Vercel execution and unbuilt kits were not measured. Do not turn these figures into a production latency promise.

The original scheduler estimate that 40 slides cannot fit a generation step is not established by these local render measurements. The real deadline risk is model time plus storage round trips under the tick abort. Measure those together before choosing eager warming.

Using the repository pricing assumptions, expected Sonnet copy cost is about $0.024/post or $2.88 for 120 posts. The token-budget arithmetic maximum is $0.13/job for two attempts. The ledger reserves $0.25 while unsettled and counts actual recorded cost after settlement; uncertain provider failures can still book $0.25 for 24 hours. Generation stays under $1/rolling day and eight jobs; collection under $0.25/Prague day and $0.04/profile. These limits remain unchanged. This is an estimate, not an invoice or evidence of a live generated post.

The separate research plan has $126 of task caps under a $150 ceiling and needs owner approval. Pipeline Instagram collection is off. Vision, paid reranking and regeneration remain proposals, with unmeasured quality. Model-price assumptions come from the pinned code and audit, not a fresh provider price check.

## Consolidation recommendation

Keep option (a), the pinned upstream vendor, while implementing the accepted additive kit engine. Derive tables from its registry and retain golden and vendor checks. Forking only DNESKAi still pulls about 81–83% of the studio and option (b) leaves Quorum consumers and tests behind. A permanent move or fork needs the owner’s consolidation decision. No files have been relocated.
