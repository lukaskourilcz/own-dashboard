# Oslo developer job research

Researched 16 October 2026. This public register contains paraphrased source summaries and profile-match assessments; private cover letters are stored only in the owner account. Scores are not hiring probabilities.

Stack /40 + experience /25 + domain /15 + location /10 + language /10. Mandatory Norwegian caps the total at 49%; other documented mandatory barriers cap it at 59%. Priority: at least 75%, current research, no flagged blocker and full-time rather than alternative contracts. Recheck every original before applying.

LinkedIn cutoff: 11 September 2026 (five weeks). Twelve newly saved jobs had individually observed ages of 2 days to 3 weeks. Attensi was already saved and verified at one week. Six Robotics only showed a repost date and was not saved there. Employer-site roles can be older while still listed. Duplicate employer/agency adverts are flagged rather than counted as separate hiring chances.

## Letter review

Preserve the personal greeting, warmth, programming motivation and relevant DevShark connection. Lead with two concrete examples matched to each role. Condense course and contract chronology; remove repeated live-coding offers and generic confidence statements. Replace stale Prague residence wording with the planned October 22 move, then update it after arrival. Name n8n, LangChain, Apify and TypeSafe AI (Jev) for AI integration roles. Distinguish integration from model training, React from Svelte/SignalR learning, and C# familiarity from production expertise. Never invent a degree, tenure or clearance eligibility.
