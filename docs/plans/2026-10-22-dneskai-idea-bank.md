## C. Caption and CTA patterns from the genre

1. Keyword inside the first 125 characters of the caption and repeated on the last slide; vendor data puts the
   trigger rate at 25 to 55 % against 5 to 12 % below the fold. https://www.replyrush.com/post/instagram-keyword-trigger
2. A concrete noun as keyword (SYSTEM, CHECKLIST, AUDIT), never TIPS, INFO, FREE or YES. Same source.
3. The first DM delivers exactly what the caption promised; the reader has to answer inside the 24-hour window for
   any follow-up. https://creatorlanehq.com/blog/instagram-comment-to-dm-automation-2026-guide
4. "Here is my system" plus a receipt (a number). https://www.bensbites.com/p/zucks-wonderfull ; Czech example:
   https://cc.cz/vsichni-pisou-jak-jim-ai-meni-zivot-ale-malokdo-rekne-jak-tady-je-muj-system-kterym-krotim-inboxy-i-ukoly/
5. Separate "models" from "tools you can use today", and give each tool its price, limit and data policy.
   https://aisdg.substack.com/p/ai-news-for-october-16-2026
