07 · Design system changes · type, colour, grid, image ratios, card and row anatomy

Keep the tokens, change the roles

Everything below is expressed with the existing custom properties plus four new ones. What makes the site read as a publication is not new colour but a stricter division of labour between the three typefaces, one ink rule, a 12-column grid, and photographs at 3:2 and 1:1 instead of 21:9 and 4:3.

4 · Grid

Container 1360 px (unchanged), 12 columns, 24 px gutters, 40 px page padding at 1440 → 91.3 px columns. Spans used: lead 12 / 8 / 7; secondary 4 / 5; briefs 8 + aside 4; article body 8 + side 4; photo 9 + caption 3; date column 200 px fixed on list pages. Below 960 px everything is one column with 12 px page padding; the section strip scrolls. The 244 px sidebar and the 300 px right rail tokens are retired.

fallback — when an article has no photo (legacy, or a drawn plate): a #efefec box with a hairline and a mono line stating the fact, at the slot's ratio so nothing shifts. The lead slot never falls back to a box: a photo-less lead becomes a typographic lead (headline on paper at 60 px, 8 cols) and the day's next article with a photo takes the picture position. The neon SVG plates are no longer rendered anywhere.
